# ScreenGrabCraft app icons

Two icons, one for each app, both portraits of a bird in the manner of a wood engraving. They
follow the icon framing of the Craft apps.

- **ScreenGrabCraft** (the tray capture service): a **peregrine falcon**, head and shoulders,
  three-quarter view, facing left. Its dark hood, the broad "moustache" under the eye, the hooked
  beak and the big dark eye make it read even at 32 px. Four corner brackets of a capture
  selection frame its head, the way the capture overlay frames what it is about to take. The
  breast is barred in rows of engraved marks and the back and wing are rows of slate feathers.
  Both run off the right and bottom edges.
- **ScreenGrabCraft Editor** (the annotation editor): a **barn owl**, face on, with a **pencil**
  held crosswise in its beak. The heart-shaped facial disc is fine lines out from the big dark
  eyes, edged by its ruff. The crown is lines round the head with pale flecks, and the folded
  wings are rows of feathers like the MailCraft pigeon's wing. The pencil is faceted, sharpened to
  a point on the left, with a ferrule and eraser on the right.

## Palette

Exactly three colours in each icon:

| Colour | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0b0b0c` | line work, the falcon's hood, the eyes, the pencil's shadowed face |
| Paper | `#efe9dc` | the birds, the selection brackets, the pencil |
| ScreenGrabCraft teal (the capture app's colour) | `#0e7c7b` | the full-bleed field of the falcon |
| ScreenGrabCraft Editor indigo (the editor's colour) | `#4e3ca8` | the full-bleed field of the owl |

## Geometry

A 512-unit tile, a rounded square with `rx=112`, no border. The figure is clipped to an inner
rounded rectangle inset 6 units with `rx=106`, so a thin band of the app colour wraps it. The
macOS files pad the tile onto Apple's 824/1024 icon grid. The sizes from 16 to 64 px crop 22
units off each side so the figure still reads.

## Where they come from

The artwork is drawn in Rust, in `xtask/src/art/`. `falcon.rs` and `owl.rs` write out the curves,
the shading lines and the feathers, and `pen.rs` holds the pen and paper they are drawn with
(lines, cubic Béziers and arcs, filled and stroked with tiny-skia). Each drawing is made at
4096 px in a 1000-unit square and snapped to ink, paper and field. Nothing is traced, copied or
generated by an image model, and no other product's imagery is used. It is original work of the
ScreenGrabCraft contributors, under the project's licence (MIT OR Apache-2.0).

`cargo xtask icon` draws both and fits each to the tile, then writes every file here, for each
of `screengrabcraft` and `screengrabcraft-editor`:

- the `hicolor` PNGs for Linux (`io.github.billsecond.screengrabcraft.png` and
  `io.github.billsecond.screengrabcraft.editor.png`, 16 to 512 px);
- `<name>.ico` for Windows (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px);
- `<name>.icns` and `<name>-macos-512.png` for macOS;
- `<name>-1024.png`.

## Changing them

Edit the drawings in `xtask/src/art/`, then:

```sh
cargo xtask icon
```
