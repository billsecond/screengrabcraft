English word list for spell checking in ScreenGrabCraft
=======================================================

The file frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt is SymSpell's English frequency dictionary, by
Wolf Garbe, as released at
https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell/blob/master/SymSpell/frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt
(SHA-256 c604e1121e398ae7c7fbf777f11e0a0f2fa66eda932cb9fba1321466cf3acd7b), unmodified, under
the MIT License below.

SymSpell made it by keeping the words found in both of these:

- Google Books Ngram data, for how often each word is used: Google, the Google Books Ngram
  Viewer datasets, http://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv2.html, under the
  Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.
- SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), for which words are English: Kevin Atkinson and
  others, http://wordlist.aspell.net/, whose notice is below.

The words are checked by symspell, the Rust crate by René Klačan
(https://github.com/reneklacan/symspell), under the MIT License.
